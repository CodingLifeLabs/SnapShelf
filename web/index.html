The core loop

One keystroke to a shelf that files itself

  1. Capture

    Every screenshot lands on the shelf the moment it appears. No drag, no rename.

  2. Shelve

    Recent captures rest on a floating shelf. Hover to copy, drag anywhere, pin what matters.

  3. AI names it

    On-device models turn pixels into a real name — “Stripe invoice error”, not IMG_4471.

  4. Organize

    Smart folders sort by app and subject. Recordings file themselves by month.

  5. Search

    OCR read every capture. Type what the screenshot showed, not what it was called.

  6. Archive

    Pin, note, dedupe, or trash. Duplicates detected by pixel hash — keep the best one.

FAQ

Straight answers

What permissions does it need?

Screenshots made by SnapShelf need screen recording. Folder watching works on the app’s own library without any prompt. Clipboard history is local-only and can be switched off.

Does it work offline?

Capture, shelf, OCR, search, dedupe and organizing are fully offline. AI naming defaults to the on-device model; cloud providers are opt-in.

What does the AI see?

By default, nothing — rules-based naming runs locally. With an AI provider enabled, only the image (and its OCR text) is sent to the provider you configured. Every transfer is logged on the privacy dashboard.

Which Macs are supported?

macOS 14 Sonoma or newer, Apple Silicon and Intel. On-device AI naming requires an Apple Silicon Mac.

How much does it cost?

Free during beta. A one-time Pro license later — no subscription.
